HANDICAP CALCULATIONS

Implement correct WHS maths.

Use:

Course Handicap =
Handicap Index × (Slope Rating / 113)
+ (Course Rating - Par)

Then:

Playing Handicap =
Course Handicap × allowance %

Allowances:

Stroke Play:
95%

Stableford:
100%

Match Play:
100%

Fourball:
85%

50%
